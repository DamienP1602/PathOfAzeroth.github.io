Patch 1.1.0 : Blackrock Update !


New Content

Spells & Stats

  • New Mage Spells : Arcane Intellect, Frost Bolt
  • New Hunter Spells : Aspect of the Hawk, Raptor Strike
  • New Priest Spells : Power Word : Fortitude, Shadow Word : Pain
  • New : All creatures and players have new resistances : Fire, Frost, Arcane, Nature, Shadow.
    • If a creature or a Player have more than 0 resistances, damages will be reduced but if it's less than 0, damages will be increased.
  • New : Players now have passive health regeneration increased by Spirit.

Classes

  • New : T0 armor are now restricted to classes, it wont' gives statistics if Players don't have the right class :
    • Chain of the Scarlet Crusade : Warrior
    • Vestments of the Devout : Priest
    • Magister's Regalia : Mage
    • Beaststalker Armor : Hunter

UI

  • New : There's now 2 stats UI, normal & advanced
  • New : For equipping a spell, you can now click on the spell and it will be auto equipped at the first available slot. Clicking on a spell slot will desequip the spell.
  • New : 2 new Spell slots
  • New : Spell Casting Bar

Content

  • New : Players have now coins divided between copper, silver and gold coins. You can obtain coins by killing mobs.
  • New : There's now 2 gamerule showing gold and reputation gains.
  • New : New hub : Blackrock Mountain
  • New : 2 new Dungeons : Blackrock Depths & Upper Blackrock Spire

Changes

Items

  • Malicious Axe : 6 Stamina, 8 Attack Power >>> 12 Stamina, 4 Attack Power
  • Imperial Chest Set : 4 Attack Power >>> 2 Attack Power
  • Cursed Felblade : 10 ilv >>> 11 ilv
  • Mirah's Song : 7 Agility, 7 Strength >>> 9 Agility, 7 Strength
  • Pelenope's Rose : 5 Intellect, 5 Stamina, 4 Spirit >>> 6 Intellect, 5 Stamina, 5 Spirit
  • Shard of Primordial Fire >>> Blackrock Mountain (It won't start a quest)
  • T0 Priest Set now drops from bosses in Blackrock Depths.

Spells & Talents

  • Some spells now deals elemental damages :
    • Fireball, Flamestrike, Flamestrike DoT >>> Fire Damage
    • Colf of Cold >>> Frost Damage
    • Arcane Shot >>> Arcane Damage
    • Mind Blast >>> Shadow Damage
    • Serpent Sting DoT, Thunderclap >>> Nature Damage
  • Nature Damage
  • Shadow Weakness : Increase damage received by Mind Blast by 25%. >>> Reduces the Shadow Resistances by 15.
  • Lightning Reflexes : Increases your Agility by 3/6/9/10/15%. >>> 2/4/6/8/10%.
  • Toughness : Increases your Stamina by 3/6/9/12/15%. >>> 2/4/6/8/10%.
  • Spiritual Healing : Increases your Spirit by 5/10/15/20/25%. >>> 2/4/6/8/10%.
  • Improved Healing : Increase the healing done by your Flash Heal spell by 3/6/9/12/15% of your Spirit. >>> 2/4/6/8/10%. (Not on Spirit)
  • Shadow Focus : Increases the damages dealt by your Mind Blast spell by 5/10/15/20/25% of your Spirit.>> 3/6/9/12/15%. (Not on Spirit)
  • Spell descriptions now shows the casting time of a spell.
  • Overall changement on how item descriptions works.

Graphics

  • Rework of Raging Berserker's Helm & Lionheart Helm model.

Fixes

  • In Patch 1.0.1, Life Drain spell was "fixed" but wasn't, it would still crash the game because the mob casting the spell would regain health when dead and it would be in a strange state where it's still alive but red and on the ground, hitting him would crash the game. Now it should be fixed.
  • Bows won't put the main hand item in cooldown forever if you have one in your off-hand. They will now take over the main-hand item if you have selected a spell launched by a bow, otherwise you will cast the spell with your main-hand weapon.
